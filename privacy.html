Cat Grid privacy policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026. Publisher: mohamed lounnas (Physia.dev on Google Play).

Your game stays on your device

Cat Grid does not require an account. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information, and includes no advertising, analytics, tracking or in-app purchases. Puzzles, fonts, artwork and sound generation are bundled in the app. Daily puzzles are generated on your device.

Local storage and deletion

The app stores your level, points, puzzle marks, rewards, language, daily streak and settings in local device storage. We do not receive this data. Use Settings → Reset progress to reset game progress, or remove the app and its data through your device settings to delete all local data. Your operating system may include app data in device backups according to your backup settings.

Device features

Sound is generated locally. Optional vibration follows the game's settings. We do not request access to contacts, camera, microphone or location.

Children

The same privacy practices apply to all players. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or other players.

Support and external links

You can contact the publisher through the project support page. Information you choose to post there is handled by GitHub under its own privacy policy. Do not post private information in a public issue. App-store downloads, payments (if applicable to your store account), diagnostics and device backups are handled by Apple or Google under their policies; they are not collected by Cat Grid.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the store disclosures will be updated.
